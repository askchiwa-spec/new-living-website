Who we are

A neighborhood agency, licensed to do serious work.

New Living Health Care Services is a Core Service Agency licensed and certified by the District of Columbia and accredited by the Council on Accreditation to provide behavioral health and supportive services.

We work to support our clients’ success in the community and to reduce the risk of hospitalization through medication management, therapy, and other supportive services. Most of what we do happens where people actually live — at home, at work, in the neighborhood — not only in an office.

More about the agency

What we offer

Four core services, certified by the Department of Behavioral Health.

Medicaid or private insurance may cover these services for eligible District residents.

Diagnostic Assessment

A thorough look at your history and current situation, resulting in a report that names your strengths as well as your needs.

Medication & Somatic Treatment

Prescribing, monitoring, and medical review by qualified clinical staff, including lab work related to your medication.

Counseling

Individual, group, and family sessions focused on managing symptoms and rebuilding daily living skills.

Community Support Services

Practical help in the community — housing, benefits, appointments, daily routines — from a worker who knows you.

How it starts

Three steps, and none of them are complicated.

You can refer yourself. A family member, a hospital, a shelter, a case manager, or a court can also refer you. You do not need a diagnosis in hand before you call.

Call 202-248-1356

You call, or someone calls for you

We take some basic details, check your insurance or Medicaid eligibility, and book your first appointment. Office hours are Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

We complete a diagnostic assessment

A clinician sits with you and builds a full picture — physical, family, social, psychiatric. This determines which services you qualify for and will benefit from.

Your services begin, and your team stays with you

You are matched with the right combination of medication management, counseling, and community support — and with the people who will deliver it.
