Become a CSW

Become a Community Support Worker.

The CSW role is where this agency meets the neighborhood — and it is open at entry level.

Community Support Workers deliver practical help where our consumers actually live: housing, benefits, appointments, daily routines, and the follow-through that decides whether treatment holds. You are trained, supervised, and part of a multidisciplinary clinical team from day one.

If you are starting out in behavioral health — or you know this community and want your work to count in it — this is the role to apply for.
