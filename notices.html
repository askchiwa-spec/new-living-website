New Living Health Care Services, LLC values the privacy of your communications and is committed to safeguarding your SMS contact and content data. We do not sell or share SMS contact or content data with third parties.

Types of data collected

When you communicate with New Living Health Care Services, LLC via SMS, we may collect your phone number and the content of your messages.

How this information is used

Solely to respond to your inquiries and provide customer support. We will not use your SMS data for any other purpose without your written consent.

Protection of SMS data

New Living Health Care Services, LLC employs industry-standard security measures to protect SMS data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. We are dedicated to ensuring the confidentiality and integrity of your SMS communications.

Disclosure of SMS data

We do not disclose SMS contact or content data to third parties unless required by law, or necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of New Living Health Care Services, LLC, its users, or others. SMS consent is not shared with third parties.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your SMS contact or content data, except where retention is required by law. For assistance or to exercise your rights, contact us at 202-248-1356.

Retention policy

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, including legal, accounting, and reporting obligations. For tax purposes we are required to retain basic customer information (contact, identity, financial, and transaction data) for six years after a customer relationship ends. In some circumstances we may anonymize your personal data for research or statistical purposes, in which case the information may be used indefinitely without further notice.

Changes to this policy

New Living Health Care Services, LLC reserves the right to update or modify this SMS Privacy Policy at any time. Changes become effective immediately upon posting the revised policy on the website. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Contact us

If you have questions or concerns about this SMS Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, contact us at 202-248-1356.
